PRIVACY POLICY — Sacred Verses
Last updated: 4 October 2026

WHAT THIS APP DOES
Sacred Verses shows you one verse from the world's scriptures every morning,
as a phone notification. All verses are stored inside the app — it works
fully offline and needs no account.

DATA WE COLLECT
None. We do not create accounts, do not ask for your name, email, or phone
number, and do not track what you read. Your saved (favorite) verses are
stored only on your phone, and never leave it.

NOTIFICATIONS
The app sends one daily notification with the verse of the day. You can
change the time or switch it off inside the app. On Android 13 and above,
the app asks your permission before sending notifications.

LOCKSCREEN WALLPAPER
If enabled (it is by default), the app sets your lock-screen wallpaper each
morning to a beautiful image of the day's verse. Your previous wallpaper is
replaced — switch this off in the app's settings any time to keep your own
wallpaper.

SPONSORED CONTENT
The app may show clearly-labeled sponsored recommendations (for example,
books related to the scriptures). These links may be affiliate links: if
you buy something after tapping one, we may earn a small commission at no
extra cost to you. Tapping a sponsored link opens your web browser, and
that website has its own privacy policy.

ADVERTISING (FUTURE VERSIONS)
Future versions of the app may show ads served by Google AdMob. Google may
collect your advertising ID and device information to show and measure ads.
You can opt out of personalized ads in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.
Learn more at https://policies.google.com/privacy

PERMISSIONS THE APP USES
- Notifications: to deliver the daily verse.
- Run at startup: to re-schedule the daily verse after your phone restarts.
- Exact alarms (only if you grant it): to deliver the verse at the exact
  time you chose.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
If this policy changes, the updated version will be included inside the app.
